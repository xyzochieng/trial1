Our Commitment

Responsible mineral sourcing

Africa holds some of the world’s richest mineral resources. PrimeLedger’s Ethical Mineral Mandate helps ensure they are developed and traded with integrity, environmental care and respect for human rights.

We work with mining companies, traders, governments and investors to build and enforce responsible sourcing frameworks aligned with recognised international standards.

Partner With Us

Standards we align to

  • UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights

  • LBMA Responsible Sourcing standards
  • ISO 14001 Environmental Management
  • African Mining Vision (AMV) Framework
